Colors

Points for colors can only be counting during the Hand counting phase

Costly Colors! (+6 points)

The namesake of this game, achieving a traditional "flush" of 4 cards will give you the most points possible with one item in hand scoring.

4 in Color, 3 in Suit (+5 points)

Having 3 cards that have the same suit, and all being the same color (red or black) will achieve 5 points. This is essentially like having costly colors, except one of the cards is a different suit (but same color of course).

4 in Color, 2 in Suit (+4 points)

This basically is just having 4 cards of the same color, worth exatly double of having 3 cards of the same color.

3 in Suit (+3 points)

Having 3 cards of the same suit, but having one card of the opposite color

3 in Color (+2 points)

The most basic of color categories, just 3 cards of the same color, by virtue this also means ONLY 2 cards can have the same suit.



Pairs and Prials

Points for having pairs, prials, or double prials, can be scored during pegging, and in hand play.

Pair (+2 points)

A pair is having 2 of the same type (value) card.

Prial (+9 points)

A prial just means having "three-of-a-kind". It delineates historically from the term "Pair Royal", which eventually became shortened / a portmanteu to "Pri-al".

Double Prial (+18 points)

4-of-a-kind. I do not know why this is called a "Double" Prial, but history has gave us this term.
